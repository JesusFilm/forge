# Credits block

Every published devotional names the film, the Bible translation and the reflection's human source with its publication year. No AI disclaimer line in social copy (owner decision; the platforms do not require it for this content).

## Short form (Instagram, Facebook)

```
Film: The JESUS Film, Jesus Film Project
Scripture: Berean Standard Bible (public domain)
Reflection: adapted from J.C. Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels, 1858
```

## Long form (YouTube description)

```
CREDITS
Film: The JESUS Film (1979), Jesus Film Project
Scripture: Luke 18:14, Berean Standard Bible (public domain)
Reflection: adapted from J.C. Ryle, "Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Luke" (1858), public domain
```

- Name the film that is actually in the video (LUMO: The Gospel of Luke, or the JESUS film).
- Name the translation the language uses (English: BSB; Russian: Синодальный перевод).
- Translate the block for other languages; names and years stay.

## Parallel passages line

When the story is also told in another Gospel, add one line under the description: "Also in Matthew 20:29–34 and Mark 10:46–52." / «Эта история есть также у Матфея (20:29–34) и Марка (10:46–52).» Same event only; skip single-Gospel stories.
