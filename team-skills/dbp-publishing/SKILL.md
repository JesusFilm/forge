# Daily Bible Pause: publishing

Each language team publishes its own variant on its own channels. Fill in the team values in `references/team-setup.md` first.

## Posting rhythm

- Long forms on {{LONG_FORM_DAYS}} (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri) at {{LONG_FORM_TIME}}, with the intro Short the same day.
- Up to 3 Shorts on the other days, at most one per story per day.
- Weekend posts are scheduled by Friday.
- Facebook: at most one reel or post a day, never a repost. Instagram and YouTube take the rest.
- Spread the load evenly: no heavy day followed by an empty one.
- After publishing, report the row (story, kind, platforms, date and time, title, links) to whoever keeps the story calendar.

## YouTube long form

1. Check you are on the right channel.
2. Covers: set the **Title and thumbnail** test first (3 covers, each with its own title), then the description. Rotate which cover type leads (face / bold words / story moment) so the channel grid varies.
3. **Title** = search phrase + hook, then ` | <passage>`; the phrase in the first ~40 characters; every word literally true. Never "devotional" or "The Chosen" in English titles.
4. **Description**: first two lines = the search phrase, the story's other common names and the passage; then the text; then the parallel-passages line if the story is in other Gospels; then the credits.
5. **Pinned comment**: a link to this episode's scene on jesusfilm.org, e.g. "Watch this scene and the whole JESUS film here 👉 <url>", with UTM `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=pinned_comment&utm_campaign={{UTM_CAMPAIGN}}&utm_content=<story_slug>`.
6. Playlist {{PLAYLIST}}; not made for kids; paid promotion No; altered or synthetic content No; no AI disclaimer line.
7. Card at 0:00 to the channel; a playlist card where the prayer ends (last subtitle cue); end screen 20 s: Subscribe + playlist.
8. Publish, then record the link.

Do not edit the title or thumbnail while a Test & compare is running: it deletes the test.

## YouTube Shorts

No test, no card, no end screen, no pinned comment, never in the long-form playlist. Description = caption, blank line, `#Shorts` + hashtags, blank line, credits. After saving, set **Related video** to the story's long form. Mix question and statement titles.

## Facebook and Instagram

Same caption text for both. No "write Amen" or other comment prompts (Meta demotes them). One Facebook post a day; when a day has an insight Short, it takes the Facebook slot.

## Other platforms

Before using a platform not listed here (e.g. Rutube, VK), check whether it can be automated at all and what it requires; until then publish by hand with the same title, description and credits.
