# Draft checklist (run before the owner sees a script)

These are the misses the owner has had to catch by hand. Check every one.

## Opening

- [ ] 15 s or less (about 30 words in English, 25 in Russian). Count the words.
- [ ] Starts with one direct question in the viewer's terms, hooking as hard as the reflection's best line.
- [ ] One line of setup, then the film. No "In this devotional, we..." paragraph.
- [ ] No answer given away: the puzzle is posed, the reflection resolves it.

## Reflection

- [ ] Opens on a hook (the most surprising detail), told through the people; the key detail is not saved for the end.
- [ ] ONE line of thought; no paragraph that retells the scene the viewer just watched.
- [ ] ONE commentary point, unfolded over two or more paragraphs.
- [ ] ONE insight (history or language) in one block of 70 to 140 words, or none.
- [ ] No source names, no Greek or Hebrew words, no references to scenes outside the clip.
- [ ] Every claim literally true to the passage; claims scoped to the story.
- [ ] No worn moves; does not sound like the last two episodes.
- [ ] Verse cards (with address) wherever the text points at a verse.
- [ ] ‖ pauses after weighty sentences; a longer pause before the conclusion.
- [ ] Direction tag on every paragraph; the first one calm and plain.

## Ending

- [ ] Conclusion gathers the piece and inspires; not a step, not a summary.
- [ ] Closing verse: exact BSB text, read in context, about 35 words or fewer, not echoing the reflection word for word.
- [ ] Question: one, personal, not yes/no.
- [ ] Prayer: asks only what a person can sincerely ask.

## Whole script

- [ ] No em or en dashes.
- [ ] Runs 8:00 or less (shorten the script, never speed it up).
- [ ] Shorts and captions in the same doc match the final text.
