# Daily Bible Pause: Shorts

Shorts are cut from a finished long form. They are proposed together with the script, in the story's Claude Doc, and approved with it.

## Which Shorts (max 4 per story)

| Short                                  | When                                       | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Intro**                              | always                                     | The long form's opening, re-cut 9:16, ending on a calm call to action: "Watch the full devotional on our YouTube channel."                                                                                   |
| **Film clip**                          | always                                     | The scene from the film with its Bible text. Opens on a short question card, closes on a turn card; key words large.                                                                                         |
| **Insight**                            | only if the story has a sharp one          | The history or language insight as an animated explainer. Use a look the audience has NOT seen in recent episodes.                                                                                           |
| **Reflection** or **Verse of the day** | one of the two, alternating story to story | Reflection: the strongest recorded lines glued into a 25 to 40 s story (hook, turn, payoff) without bending the thought. Verse: 4-5 recorded lines ending on the closing verse, then the verse word by word. |

Better three good Shorts than five weak ones. In the doc, propose them as: format | exact spoken and on-screen words | visual.

## Rules for every Short

- **Literally true**: every card and line matches the passage and the long form.
- **Safe zone**: text ends by x = 920 of 1080 (the like/comment rail), nothing in the bottom ~20% or under the top status strip; keep the lower third light. Check frames, not by eye.
- **Sound**: the long form's own music bed under every Short except the film clip; the film clip carries the film's own sound from the first second to the last.
- **Film speed**: films play at normal speed in Shorts (the 1.05 speed-up is for long forms only).
- **Covers differ**: each Short of a story gets a different shot and text on its cover; compare them side by side with the Shorts already out.
- **Emphasis**: 3-4 weighty phrases set larger (the last one largest), not uniform subtitles.
- **Same set in every language**: the owner picks the set; each language team adapts its text (see `dbp-language-adaptation`).
- **Preview before a full render**: send stills of the key cards first; render after the OK.

## Building them

Studio Manager holds the devotional kit (components, music beds, project builder). Each language renders its own variant from the same project. Voices are presets in the editor; nobody needs the voice service key.
