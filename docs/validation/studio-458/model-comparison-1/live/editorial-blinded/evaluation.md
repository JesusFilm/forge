## Interpretation and decision

A promising candidate clears objective validity in all three cases within the
budget, has no material fidelity failure or unsupported QA pass, and improves the
retained weak depth/voice dimensions without new harm. Record disagreements and
human rubric decisions instead of a synthetic aggregate success percentage.
One sample per model/case cannot establish statistical superiority or general
creative acceptance. Even 3/3 promising results do not close feat-458 media proof.

Paired outcomes guide the next action, without authorizing it:

- Both models valid after the field-feedback fix: evidence for improved interface
  feedback, not proof that larger-model capability was necessary.
- Candidate better on identical context/tool tasks: limited evidence for model
  capability under this setup; do not credit a provider, prompt or context change.
- Both fail the same operation/coverage step: inspect tool representation/feedback
  and schema complexity before another prompt patch; preserve dynamic authoring.
- Both miss facts present in the supplied excerpt: source availability alone does
  not explain the error. Model attention/reasoning/interface burden remain possible.
- Missing clip/subtitle evidence: cannot be fixed by model selection. Canonical
  source preparation needs a separate bounded test; no inferred footage fallback.
- Valid proposals but weak/overconfident prose: creative reasoning and truthful QA
  remain open even if structured tool use improves.
- Token cutoff, guard limit or transport failure: report its exact cause; do not
  relabel it as editorial failure and do not replay.
