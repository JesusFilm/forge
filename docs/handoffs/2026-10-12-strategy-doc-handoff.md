## Where things stand (Oct 12, 2026)

- Channels: YouTube EN (Daily Bible Pause, ~36 subscribers), YouTube RU (opened Oct 7, 9 subscribers), Facebook page + Instagram (daily.biblepause, ~80 followers). Lyuba runs all of them; a marketer colleague runs the paid ads.
- Output: ~3 long devotionals a week (EN + RU), 1–2 Shorts a day per story type (intro, film-scene verse, reflection, Vox insight). Made with an AI pipeline (scripts, voices, edit) and checked by Lyuba.
- What the numbers say (28 days to Oct 11): Shorts bring 84% of YouTube views and 31 of 35 new subscribers. Long videos get 3–15 organic views each. The best Short (film scene + viewer question) had 1,243 views, with 53% not swiping away. In its first week, the Russian channel had a Short with 1,273 views and 47% not swiping away, as strong as the best English one. Facebook reels: 175–550 views, 3–7 s average watch; a cliffhanger teaser held 25 s. Ads: only one (Light, $24) brought viewers who actually watched.
