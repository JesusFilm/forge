## What To Build

1. Group timeline items under Video, Audio, Text, in that order. Allow multiple visible rows for simultaneous items, especially overlapping text; keep selection and trim/move behavior.
2. Treat grouping as a presentation concern where possible. Preserve stored track order and render stacking when presenting legacy projects with mixed tracks. A text item must remain distinguishable from footage.
3. Dragging the vertical playhead or its handle continuously seeks the preview. Handle pointer capture, cancellation, scrolling, zoom, and clamping to valid frames. Provide keyboard frame navigation with an accessible label and current value.
4. Below preview show only play/pause; move time/frame display and position controls to the timeline. Preserve keyboard play/pause and go-to-start behavior.
5. New text goes into the Text group; new footage into Video; audio/music into Audio. Do not automatically split video audio.
